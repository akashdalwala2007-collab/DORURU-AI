## Attendance Requirements (Sample Data)
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in theory lectures and 80% attendance in practical sessions to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations. Condonation for medical reasons is subject to committee approval.

## Evaluation Breakdown (Sample Data)
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)**: 40% (Includes quizzes, assignments, and mid-term assessments)
- **Semester End Examination (SEE)**: 60%
- **Passing Criterion**: Minimum 40% aggregate in CIE and SEE combined, with at least 35% in SEE.

## Re-evaluation & Grievance (Sample Data)
Applications for photocopy and re-evaluation of answer scripts must be submitted to the Controller of Examinations within 7 working days from the date of result declaration.
